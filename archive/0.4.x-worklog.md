## 0.4.1 — review fixes: group kinematics, CPU/GPU parity, family tagging

Review of the initial slice against the plan, driven by the visual bug report
"the bar is slower on orbits than stars near it" (and a list of latent parity
defects found while reproducing it):

**The reported bug and the fix (plan §1.1a, new).** The bar already rotated
rigidly at Ω_p, but every disc star *inside corotation* (R_CR = vFlat/Ω_p ≈ 5.5 kpc
on the MW preset) circulates at Ω(R) — up to 5.5× the pattern speed — so the bar
visually lagged the stars mixed into and around it. Fix: the **group zone** — the
disc family rotates at Ω_p inside R_CR (continuous there, since Ω(R) crosses Ω_p
exactly at the seam), differential outside; Ω_p = 0 types (S0, E, Irr) never lock.
Bar + arms + inner disc now turn as one group. Cost: one `select` — group speed is
the same closed form with a shared ω, so the §4 budget does not move. Bar-family
wobble was zeroed in the tables (rigid body; the 2:1 loop is a later refinement).

**Parity defects fixed (each would have shown as unreal motion):**

- The WGSL hard-coded the Milky Way's numbers (0.041 / 0.225 / 0.5 and its own
  pressure clock ×0.1 off) while the CPU mirror read `model.dynamics` — every
  non-MW type drew with MW rotation and labels diverged from stars. The uniform
  now carries `dynA`/`dynB` (8 floats packed by `orbit.packOrbitDynamics`;
  112 → 144 bytes; all defaults applied CPU-side so the shader hard-codes
  nothing — `wgsl-validate` enforces this).
- The shader reduced only the bulk θ before `sin` and reused it for the epicycle
  arguments — every wobble phase snapped once per revolution. Both sides now
  reduce the **full** argument (`sinTau`), keeping epicycles continuous across
  bulk wraps (orbit-test probes ±ε around a wrap).
- Wobble amplitudes disagreed 15× (CPU 0.12 vs GPU 0.008 kpc scale) and pressure
  had wobble on one side only. One law both sides: disc `A = rank·2σThin/κ(R)`

  computed family from spectral class — G stars' labels drifted off their own

- Object members got an explicit family (age < 100 Myr → pattern, globulars →

- Nebula billboards never moved although the plan requires gas to follow the

## 0.4.2 — 2026-09-22 — Half the stars frozen: bar λ / ω̄ data + law fix

**Report:** "bug not fixed — especially seen on SB0 and S0, half stars almost
not move." Diagnosis by direct probe of `fillDynamics` + `omegaFor`:

- **SB0 froze its own bar**: `buildTypeSpecs` derives SB0 from S0 with
  `Object.assign({}, base, { barred, profile, axes })` — `omegaPattern` stayed
  **0**, so the rigid bar family (and the group zone, R_CR = vFlat/0 = ∞)
  sat at ω = 0 while the disc sheared past it. Spiral barred types were fine
  only because Sa–Sd carry Ω_p = 0.025.
- **Every disc type froze its spheroid/halo**: `spinLambda: 0.0` on
  S0 / SBb-preset / Sc-base (Sa–Sd share Sc's object) — the pressure family's
  bulk spin is λ·ω̄ = **0**, leaving only the sub-pixel boil. On S0/SB0 the
  spheroid+bar *is* most of the central light → "half the stars".
- **ω̄ shape was wrong for disc types**: 0.4.1's always-Keplerian
  `0.05/r^1.5` contradicts plan §1.1's family table, which defines ω̄ as the
  mean *disc* frequency (Keplerian only for E, which has no disc curve).
- **Pattern stars froze on zero-pattern types**: S0/E/Irr give bright A
  stars ω_p = 0.

**Fixes** (model data + law + shader mirror, one contract):

- `galaxy.js`: barred derivation gets its own `dynamics` copy with
  `omegaPattern = base || BAR_OMEGA_PATTERN (0.031 = 30 km/s/kpc, §3 floor)`
  → SB0: bar rigid at 0.031, R_CR ≈ 7.42 kpc, inner disc joins the group.
  `spinLambda: 0.15` for S0, SBb preset, Sc base (plan λ ∈ 0.05–0.3; E4
  0.15 / Irr 0.30 already complied).
- `orbit.js` / `shaders.js`: hybrid `pressureClock(dyn, r)` —
  `vFlat > 0 ? vFlat/max(r,rCore) : 0.05·r^-1.5` — drives pressure bulk (×λ),
  boil frequency and the virial amplitude at 1 kpc; pattern family falls back
  to local Ω(r) when ω_p = 0 (bar never lands there without a pattern).
  No uniform growth: both new branches read dynA only.
- Resulting probe: S0 pressure ω(1.5 kpc) = 0.023 (period ≈ 273 Myr),
  pattern = Ω(r); SB0 bar/pattern/inner-disc = 0.031 as one group.

**Tests:** orbit 52/52 (new §4b sweeps *every* type for ω > 0 per used

## 0.4.3 — 2026-09-22 — The bar streams: derived group speed + x1 loops

**Report:** "bug not fixed. especially seen on sb0 — bar rotate as rigid shape.
stars on different distance from center has same angular speed — this is impossible.
[...] bar and belt should not correspond to static speed but some group speed of
stars but not individual star. can we fast emulate this?"

The 0.4.1 group zone (inner disc locked to Ω_p) and the 0.4.2 data patch both kept
the thing being reported: one rigid angular speed for bar + arms + belt. Diagnosis
was structural, not a data gap — and the research says the 0.4.1 reading was wrong,
not just the numbers. A bar is a density wave: the *pattern* is rigid, the stars are
not. They ride closed orbits whose apsides precess at Ω − κ/2 (Kormendy's
introduction; Harsoula et al. 2021 for precessing ellipses as the building blocks),
and the pattern speed is what that precession averages to over the population that
carries the pattern.

**Design (plan §1.1/§1.1a/§1.5 rewritten in place):**

- **Pattern speed is derived, never authored.** `galaxy.js.patternSpeed` =
  `(1 − 1/√2)·⟨Ω⟩` — the same κ = √2·Ω relation the epicycle law uses — read where
  the pattern is set: at the **bar's end** for a barred model (the fast-bar
  condition; a mass-weighted body average is dominated by the inner kpc and lands
  2–3× high), and as the thin disc's **mass-weighted mean** for the arms. No arms and
  no bar → 0 (no pattern family at all). The authored `omegaPattern` fields and
  `BAR_OMEGA_PATTERN` are deleted; SBb (the MW preset) has an unbarred spheroid and
  simply has no bar dynamics.
- **Derived values land in the observed bands**: SB0 40.7, SBa 31.8, SBb 43.0,
  SBc 37.9, SBd 53.0 km/s/kpc (observed 33–55; the MW's own bar 33–41), unbarred
  Sa–Sd 11–20, Irr 5.3. Corotation comes out at 3.4 bar lengths for every barred
  type, beyond the bar end by construction.
- **The corotation lock is gone.** `disc` runs at Ω(r) = vFlat/max(r, rCore)
  everywhere — the belt is the *local group rate* of the stars at that radius.
  `patternLock` survives as the documented cosmetic rigid variant.
- **The bar's stars stream through the pattern**: seat on the pattern at Ω_p plus an
  x1 loop at `Ω_stream = Ω(r) − Ω_p` (plan §1.5). One extra sin pair, shaped exactly
  like the disc's existing epicycle; the disc branch loses the lock's select, so the
  net vertex cost is unchanged.
- **Loop amplitude measured, not guessed**: 0.15 · `pressureAmpScale` (4.5% of the
  bar's semimajor axis). A 400k-star SB0 sweep in the bar's own frame showed the
  90% outline of the bar stars drifting 0.6–1.4% over 400 Myr for loop fractions
  0.2–0.5 and 0.2–0.5% at 0.15, so shape damage is small at either end and the
  fraction is a visibility/shape trade. 0.15 is the shipped value: half the outline
  drift of 0.25 for streaming that is still visible at 0.5–5 Myr/s (the browser pass
  is the gate on whether it needs to go back up). Loop amplitude is per-star (rank);
  the *rate* is per radius — the "group speed, not individual star" requirement.

**What the visual harness shows** (`/tmp/visual.js`, 400k stars, face-on density in
the bar's own frame, PNG per time): with the 0.4.2 law the bar's closed orbits never
stream (rigid, the bug); with the 0.4.3 law the bar's stars visibly circulate inside
the bar's outline while the outline itself holds, and the disc outside the bar shows
its ring shells where the streaming rate falls through rational values. Before/after
against the old law on SB0, the whole-inner-disc rigid rotation is gone.

**Tests:** `orbit` 52 → 60 checks (derived-Ω_p arithmetic, streaming-rate profile
and its zero at corotation, pattern-sweep grouping, the embedded disc overtaking the
pattern, the bounded loop, the barred-type band sweep, and the f32 replay extended
with the bar branch). `wgsl-validate` gains the two revised mirrors (no lock on
either side; the streaming term present on both sides) and drops the lock check.
`wgsl-exec` gains the on-WGSL bar fixture: T = 0 identity on the seat, displacement
matching the CPU law to 2 pc, and the on-GPU star measurably off its rigid seat.
Validation: `all-tests` ALL PASSED, `wgsl-exec` 86/86, `wgsl-validate` PASS.

Next step: browser visual pass on the streaming bar at 0.5–5 Myr/s (the numbers are
pixel-scale there), and the amplitude-calibration gate of plan §1.4.

## 0.4.4 — wave damping is a pattern-frame capture, not a slowdown

The damping draft and the linked repo's friction field were both the wrong
sign for this disc. The draft's cosine phase-pull shears arms apart (its
radial derivative fights the pitch). The linked field (`ω · (1 − s·D)`,
archive-only, numerical) jams only inside corotation; their demo disc never
crosses it, and this one mostly sits outside, where that slowdown empties the
arms. Analysis in `archive/damping-plan-report.md`. The law that shipped is
`damping-plan.md`: `dχ/dt = α·m·(Ω−Ω_p)·sin²(χ/2)`, closed form, disc family
only, α = 0 bit-identical to the 0.4.3 shear. Sampler and `StarPacked` are
untouched. The page default is 0.6 (module default stays 0); Defaults restores
it, a type/seed/age change does not.

Uniform grows by `waveA`/`waveB` on both camera structs (nebulae ignore the
pair): 144 → 176 bytes, `UNIFORM_FLOATS` 36 → 44. Linked-repo advice (their
integrator, their single rotation) stays in the report; nothing in that tree
was changed from here.

## 0.4.5 — the simple engine (selectable friction field), honest giants, HDR knobs

Three requests landed together: a selectable star-movement engine out of the
linked friction-field demo (the classic law is not stable enough to be the only
option), a realism pass on the star-type distribution, and an HDR comparison
against the same demo. All three shipped.

**The simple engine.** The demo's `simple-friction-field-webGPU.html` is a CPU
per-star theta integrator — `rho` with an eccentric wobble, a log-spiral
distance `D`, `omega = (V0/(rho+0.2)) * (1-damping*D)` — a stable toy, not
N-body, and that is the point: it cannot blow up. The port maps it onto galaxy
types instead of demo constants. Armed spirals and barred types get the full
jam (pattern speed, pitch, sigma from the model's own arms, damping from the
shared wave slider); E/S0/Irr pack `m = 0` and orbit differentially with no
capture term. `orbit.js` carries the law (`simpleOmega`, substepped Euler with
the `dtheta` cap, `simplePositionFromTheta` as the vertex mirror) and the GPU
side is a `simple-step` compute entry over one theta f32 per slot, dispatched
before the sprites, with the vertex rebuilding positions from the integrated
azimuth when the new engine lane reads 1. The camera uniform grows to 192
bytes (44 → 48 floats); the tonemap uniform to 32 (see below). Labels, picking
and the CPU landmark mirror step beside the GPU buffer with no readback, and
the engine is a menu/URL select — classic stays the default, reset re-seeds
the epoch, an age-only regenerate does not (its GPU thetas survive).

**Honest giants.** The old population kept every post-MS star a giant forever:
3% of the reference field sat permanently on the branch, and the old field
outshone the young one 11× in mean luminosity, which the exposure calibration
then had to erase. The giant branch
is now temporary — 15% of the main-sequence life, clamped to 2 Myr … 1 Gyr —
and past `tMS*1.1 + tGiant` the star is a cooling white dwarf on an 8 Gyr
e-folding toward 4000 K / 5e-4 Lsun. Massive stars keep the instant-remnant
channel. Giants concentrate at the turnoff; the young/old gap is ~2.2× and the
young-MW offset re-pins at −0.84 (rule `< -0.5`). WGSL mirrors the branch with
the same constant names, and the census pins hold both sides: E4/S0 giant
fraction 0.10–0.12 with `gMax < 1.1` and `wd% > 2.5`, forming types `OB > 0.05%`
with `gMax > 3`, E4 remnants cooler and dimmer than SBb's.

**HDR: their present, our knobs.** The demo's present pass is a chromaticity-
preserving peak compress under a headroom ceiling — pure hues to any peak —
while ours fades bright cores to white (filmic shoulder plus highlight
desaturation) under a fixed ceiling of 8. Neither is "better"; they are looks.
So both are knobs now: headroom 1–8 (default 8, today's ceiling) and highlight
desat 0–1 (default 1, today's film look), riding the tonemap uniform's second
vec4 with menu sliders beside exposure. Desat 0 reproduces the demo's pure-blue-
to-the-peak exactly — `(0, 0, 200) → (0, 0, 8)` where the default gives white —
and omitting both knobs reproduces today's pixels bit for bit.

**Tests.** `orbit` 60 → 94 (§12: per-type arming pins, substep caps, the jam's
sign on both sides of corotation, the f32 drift probe at 2.26e-6 rad/1000 Myr).
`renderer` 106 → 119 (compute mocks, 192/32-byte re-pins, engine-lane, dispatch
and knob checks). `wgsl-validate` → 171 (simple-step shape, engine/tonemap
layout, the inertial ban rescoped to the classic extract). `hdr` → 23 (knob
properties in the mirror). `star-types-evolution` → 50 (census pins).
`wgsl-exec` 88 → 102: the simple-step kernel executes invocation by invocation

## 0.4.7 — 2026-09-26 — Apocenter-guided orbital engine

Implemented the draft as a third, opt-in engine. It treats the stable arms/bar as
prescribed apsidal features: disc/pattern stars choose the nearest spiral crest,
bar stars choose the nearest bar major-axis direction, and unfeatured families
retain their own birth azimuth. The birth radius and anomaly define a bounded
Kepler ellipse, solved analytically with five Newton steps. The apsidal direction
turns with the model's pattern speed; differential local clocks make stars dwell
at apocenter. Every orbit plane is anchored on the model centre, with orientation
from the existing deterministic inclination bits. T=0 returns the exact birth
point.

The `apocenter` engine is available in the Engine menu and `?engine=apocenter`.
It uses the existing engine uniform lane and vertex shader, with no buffer growth
or per-frame allocation. The CPU orbit mirror drives labels and selection. The
classic and simple movement laws remain in place. The preview surfaced a separate
render-target mismatch: star and nebula pipelines now target the RGBA16Float HDR
intermediate, while tonemap alone targets the swapchain. While exercising the
suite, synchronized stale 3-D test mirrors with the centre-crossing plane
implementation and made the simple pattern phase zero for reverse-time inputs, as
its tests require.

**Validation:** `python3 scripts/run.py all-tests` → ALL TESTS PASSED;
`python3 scripts/run.py wgsl-exec` → 104/104, including execution of the apocenter
vertex path against the CPU Kepler mirror; `orbit` 102/102; `renderer` 121/121;
`wgsl-validate` 172/172; `m1-smoke` 49/49.

**Known limit:** the density preservation is a hypothesis, not a measured result.

## 0.4.x — 2026-09-26 — Realistic 3-D shapes I: the bar becomes a slice family (0.4.8)

Implemented M1 of `0.4.8-plan-realistic-3d-shapes.md`. The bar field was a hard
slab in Z: `rhoSpheroid`'s bar branch kept a flat `|z| < c` profile, so any
edge-on SB* showed a rectangular rim while the reference images (NGC 128, NGC
4710) and the MW bar models show a smooth boxy/peanut silhouette. The branch is
now a slice-profile family:

- `rho_bar = amp · L(xi) · (1 − |u|^n − |v|^cv)^q` with `u = eta/tau`,
  `v = zeta/(c·r0·P·tau)`, `tau = (tip^n − |xi|^n)^(1/n)` and `P = 1 + peanut·xi^2`:
  the cross-section shrinks toward the tip (X-shape), the peanut term thickens it
  at the ends, and the vertical exponent `cv` is a new per-type authored knob
  (`bar.vertical`; `BAR_VERTICAL` anchors 2.60 → 2.00). Values under 2 put a cusp
  at the midplane (`1 − |z|^cv` has infinite slope at 0, a crease edge-on), so 2 is
  the floor; in-plane boxiness `n`, `peanut`, `plateau` and `endCap` are unchanged.
- `barSliceMass` closes the slice integral analytically
  (`4·K(cv,q)·(1/n)·B(1/n, q+1/cv+1)` through log-gamma), so `massIntegrals.bulge`
  is exact instead of Sersic-approximated, and the sampler needs no rejection:
  two 1-D Simpson tables, `p(u) ∝ (1−|u|^n)^(q+1/cv)` and `p(w) ∝ (1−w^cv)^q`
  (the vertical draw is independent of `u`). `sampleBarPoint` takes three randoms
  now, and the vertical marginal's total variation against the field is 0.030 at
  250k stars.
- Mirrored in WGSL: `barSliceProfile` inside `rhoSpheroid`, reading
  `params.barShape.w` as `cv`, with `BAR_SLICE_FALLOFF = 1.5` and `q = 1.5` on both
  sides. The bar now ends with zero gradient at its envelope, which is what
  removes the rim.
- `armFactor` ramps the pattern in over `ARM_INNER_FADE = 0.5` before
  `arms.minRadius`. The old hard switch-on was a 2.3%-of-peak step in the Irr
  field at z ≈ 0.4 — small, but exactly the kind of edge an edge-on view shows.
- New gate `experiments/shape-test.js` (69 checks; registered in `scripts/run.py`):
  a second-difference step detector over 64-bin cuts of every type, plus bar
  half-density height and peanut envelope checks. Worst residual is ≤ 0.0076 of
  the model peak (limit 0.02) and ≤ 0.0068 of the bar-cut peak (limit 0.01); SB
  half-density heights are 0.20–0.23 of the half-length; SB0/SBa carry a 1.19
  lobe/centre peanut ratio, SBc/SBd a nearly boxy 1.00–1.03.

Repins made necessary by the new field: `galaxy-types` (the n=2/cv=2 volume pin is
now the analytic slice mass, `2·a·b·c·r0³·m_slice·∫L`, and the bar-support helper
is the slice envelope `|u|^n + |v|^cv ≤ 1`), `model-parity` (the vertical marginal
is `(1−|v|^cv)^(q+1/n)`; the arm-ridge pins follow the faded arm field) and
`density-distribution`. While in the suite, reconciled the four stale engine-ID
expectations in `orbit-test`/`renderer-test` (`0/1/2` versus the shipped `1/2/4`
bit mask, and the lane's real `(mask, apocenterForce, barTilt, share)` content)

## 0.4.x — 2026-09-26 — Presets copy only the engines that are on, and the menu hides the rest

The copy/paste preset from the previous session round-tripped *every* slider, including
the ones belonging to engines the user had switched off — paste a "classic" preset into
a fresh session and it also carried an apocenter share, an eccentricity spread and a
Kepler epoch that nothing was reading. A preset should be what the galaxy *is*, not what
the sliders happen to hold.

`src/core/presets.js` now tags each field with the engine that reads it (`engine:
'apocenter'`, and so on; untagged = always active) and derives activity from the
system's own engine list: `engineListOf(state)` splits the `engine` value (`'apocenter+classic'`
→ `['classic','apocenter']`), `fieldActive(field, engines)` answers per field, and
`activeFields(state)` is what `serialize` and `describe` iterate. A classic-only system
therefore emits `FIELDS.filter(f => !f.engine)` and nothing else.

`parse` applies the same rule in reverse, and only when it can: a pasted string that
*names* its engines drops the keys those engines do not read (`engine=classic&apoShare=1&sat=2`
→ `{ engine:['classic'], sat:2 }`), while a fragment that names no engine keeps every key
it carries — the hand-off prompt's authors write partial presets, and silently dropping a
key they did mean would be worse than carrying one they didn't.

The menu now hides what it does not read: `src/index.html` tags the rows with
`data-engine`, and `syncEngine()` in `src/main.js` toggles `#menu [data-engine]` alongside
the existing engine-checkbox wiring, so the sliders appear and disappear with their
engine. Six checks added to `experiments/preset-test.js` (20/20) covering the gated
serialize, the gated and ungated parse, and `engineListOf`'s ordering.

## 0.4.x — 2026-09-26 — Realistic 3-D shapes III: no cut where the profile is still lit (0.4.8 M3)

M3 asked whether the model's hard truncations are visible. `experiments/truncation-edge.js`
(new study, `run.py truncation-edge`) measures each cut the way the eye does: the density
that disappears at the cut, divided by the brightest thing in the same view — a disc
column's own midplane, the spheroid's `s = 1` level. Three of the four were above a 1e-3
contrast floor:

| cut | worst type | before | after |
|---|---|---|---|
| disc vertical `\|z\| = discHeight` | S0 at R = 20 | 4.18e-2 | 5.33e-4 |
| spheroid `s = spheroidRadius` | E0 (Sérsic n = 4) | 5.37e-3 | 1.00e-3 |
| halo `rMax` | SB0 | 2.29e-10 | unchanged |
| disc radial `R = discRadius` | Irr | 1.03e-2 | unchanged, deliberately |

The fix is one rule, in `src/math/density.js`: a cut sits where the profile has fallen to
`TRUNCATION_FLOOR = 1e-3` of its own reference, or where the author put it — whichever is
*farther out*. `max(authored, rule)`, never `min`, so no authored galaxy ever shrinks (the
halo rule would have pulled `rMax` 100 → 7.2·a_h, and Sc's spheroid 6 → 5.14). In
scale-height units the floor is `VERTICAL_CUT_SECH2 = 8.294` for the sech² thin disc and
`VERTICAL_CUT_LAPLACE = ln(1/1e-3) = 6.908` for the Laplace thick disc; it is applied per
component and *per radius* through `discHeightAt`, so a flaring disc keeps its cut above
its own flare instead of shaving the outer column. `discVerticalCut`, `verticalCutHeights`
and `spheroidFloorRadius` are exported and mirrored in `src/render/shaders.js`
(`insideDisc(params, R, z, cutHeights, H)`), read by `sampling.js` for the z draw and by
`density.truncationFractions`/`discVerticalMass` for the mass bookkeeping. The Milky Way's
thin cut is unchanged (8.294 × 0.3 = 2.49 < the authored 3.0); its thick cut moves 3.0 →
6.2 kpc and the thick disc's delivered mass 0.958 → 0.9926. Sérsic n = 4 keeps 0.786 of its
mass instead of 0.636.

The disc's *radial* cut stays exactly as authored. A disc's outer break is an observed
feature (Pohlen & Trujillo 2006 find breaks at a few scale lengths), not an artifact of the
model's box, so the rule does not apply there; the only type that reaches the percent level
is Irr, whose cut at 5.8 scale lengths is its break and whose clump field breaks the edge up
further. An earlier version of this change did push the radial cut out to `6.908·L`, and the
compact-disc cases in `model-parity-test` caught it immediately: a test disc authored at
`discRadius = 1.2` with `L = 3.6` had its radius overridden 20×. Authored *size* is intent;
authored *slab height* was an artifact — that is the line the rule now follows.

Two rejected alternatives, both measured: a multiplicative smoothstep taper at the cut (the
taper lives in absolute `z` while the profile lives in `z/H(R)`, so the truncated CDF loses
its closed form and the z draw needs a 2-D table or per-star bisection — against the
sampler's single-pass contract), and pedestal subtraction `p(x) − p(x_cut)` (C⁰ at the cut,
but it strips 17.4% of the Sérsic n = 4 mass and 12.3% of the thick column, and still needs
bisection). Moving the cut costs nothing at runtime and buys the same darkness.

`experiments/shape-test.js` grew a §6 that guards the rule for every shipped type: each
vertical and spheroid cut ≤ 1.2 × the floor, the radial one ≤ 1.5e-2 (118/118). Three test
repairs came out of the change and are worth recording, because two of them were the tests
being wrong rather than the model: `galaxy-types` needed the group-aware `insideDisc` and a
new "no cut above the floor" check; `density-distribution`'s SBb mass shares failed at 1.9%
on a *quadrature* error, not a sampling error — a 2.3 kpc bar of steep profile inside a
40 kpc box needs more than 240×96×60 cells, and at 480×192×90 the box integral moves
0.18881 → 0.18706 against the sampler's 0.18497; `object-placement`'s HII arm-ridge check
was under-sampled — at 1200 objects only ~100 are HII regions, and the mean distance-to-arm
read 0.250 before the change and 0.374 after, while at 12000 objects the true means are
0.294 and 0.308 (a 5% shift from the component reweighting, the rest realisation noise). Both
were fixed by sampling properly, not by moving a limit.

M3.2, the young-population vertical audit, is `experiments/young-vertical.js` (new study).
The finding: the young population has **no vertical structure of its own**. The model places
a star from the component field first and types it afterwards — `deriveStar` reads
`(component, R, distToArm)` and never `z` — so the O/B stars come out of the same sech²
column as the M dwarfs: young/old scale-height ratio 1.07 for the preset and 1.07–1.16 across
Sb/Sc/Sd/Irr, the excess being the arms sitting at larger, more flared radii. Observed it is
near 0.2 (a 45–90 pc OB layer against ~300 pc). The number the model needs already exists —
`populations.youngScaleHeight`, the gas lane at half the thin-disc height — but only
`nebula.js` and `objects.js` read it, so the nebulae are in a thin lane while the stars born
in them are not. The fix is deferred rather than improvised: the right one is to couple *age*
to height (pass `z/H(R)` into `sampleLocalAge` and let the arm-young branch fall off with it)
rather than to add a second spatial sampler — that is the physical direction, discs heat as
they age, it keeps one sampler, and `procedural-gen` already has the position on the GPU
side. It changes `deriveStar`'s signature and all of its callers, so it is logged as M3.3.

## 0.4.8 M3.3 — age coupled to height, and a Milky Way arrival preset

**M3.3.** `sampleLocalAge` now takes `zOverH` (0 is the old midplane age, so every existing
pin still describes the midplane). Two changes, both using numbers the model already had:

- The arm-young gate is multiplied by a Gaussian of width `populations.youngScaleHeight`
  (the gas lane, in units of H(R)). A newborn has to be in the arm *and* in the lane.
- The field branch then takes a heating floor `YOUNG_ARM_MAX_GYR · (z/lane)²`. The arm gate
  alone does not thin the layer: the SFH's young tail plants O/B stars at every height, and
  that tail is what kept later types near ratio 1. The floor is applied to the field branch
  only — applying it to arm newborns clamps the lane edge to exactly 0.3 Gyr.

path passes it (`star-sprites.js` field and local writes, `deriveStarProps`, and
`meanFieldLuminosity`, so the exposure calibration matches the renderer). The 200k arm-hug
sample and the `galaxy-age` prior loop stay at `z = 0`: they pin the midplane, and passing
height would move both pins. The WGSL mirror is `procedural-gen`'s `sampleLocalAge`, which
already had the star's position; `clock.w` carries `youngScaleHeight` (it was the unused
