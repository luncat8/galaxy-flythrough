## 0.7.0 — 2026-10-22 — The clock stops re-rolling what is on screen

Plan §6 R3, the smoothness contract's first rule and the one confirmed jump.

**Defect.** `renderer.refreshObjects` re-placed the whole composite block on an
epoch change (`generateObjects` → `placeObjects` against the *current* model,
every member re-seeded from the new list) whenever the age had moved
`OBJECT_REFRESH_AGE = 0.02` Gyr. Cluster and HII members are records in the same
`StarPacked` buffer as the field, so at the top star rate (99 Myr/s) that re-roll
replaced a slice of the visible sky about five times a second, and every nebula
billboard jumped with it. E4 had already removed this class of defect for the
field (draw once, place by the epoch map); the object block was left behind
because its *placement* reads age-dependent acceptance rates.

**Fix.** The block is drawn with the field and carried. `refreshObjects`,
`OBJECT_REFRESH_AGE` and `state.objectsEpochAge` are gone, `setEpoch` no longer
places anything, and `regenerate`/`rebuildPopulation` remain the only callers of
`generateObjects`. Nothing is lost by it: a member's look is epoch-independent by
construction — `deriveStarWithAge` imposes the object's own age, and the
metallicity follows the component — so the refresh was re-rolling seats to
recompute bytes that would have come back identical. The gas-driven change in the
*rate* of new objects returns in 0.7.4/0.7.5 as a gate on the running epoch, not
as a re-place.

**Validated.** `renderer-test` **135/135**, with the old "an epoch change re-places
the objects, rationed by age" pin replaced by its opposite: the object block's
record bytes and member count are identical before, during and after a walk to
0.5 → 0.6 Gyr → back to the reference. `view-tests` 16/16, `changed` 7/7
(`export-parity`, `m1-smoke`, `wgsl-validate`, `preset`, `renderer`, `hdr`,
`view-modes-page`), `node --check` and `git diff --check` clean. No browser
verdict is claimed; the row is V14 in `deferred-browser-visual-plan.md`.
