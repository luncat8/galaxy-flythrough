# The vertical column: carry the birth height (0.4.9)

## Goal

Close plan.md's follow-up order item **2a** — the amplitude-calibration pass
0.4.0 §1.4 deferred — so the time-averaged *vertical* density of the movement
engines holds the field's own sech² column the way the radial profile and the
arm pattern already do. This is the stated prerequisite for trusting any change
of default engine, so it is model work with its own contract, not a browser-only
tweak. It is sandbox-safe: no density-field, sampler, preset or render-path
change.

## Evidence (2026-10-13, `experiments/movement-density-test.js`)

Measured over 0…400 Myr at the shipped view state (wave 0.6, pattern ×1, share
0.35, force 0.22), types mw/Sa/SBc, log `experiments/logs/movement-density.json`:

| metric | classic | simple | apocenter | mask 5/6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| radial worst bin | 0.002–0.005 | 0.002–0.004 | 0.005–0.011 | ≤ 0.007 |
| **vertical worst bin** | **0.041** | **0.047** | **0.019** | **0.045–0.049** |
| **mean\|z\| / birth column** | **0.715** | **0.673** | **0.873** | **0.659–0.687** |

The radial profile meets plan §1.4's 3%-per-bin budget on every lane; the
vertical column does not, and it is **compressed, never heated** — the bin
deviation is a shape error of one sign.

## Mechanism

Every engine sweeps a per-star orbital *plane* anchored at the galaxy centre
(`orbit.js: orbitPosition`, `apocenterPosition`, `simplePositionFromTheta`; the
WGSL mirrors in `SHADER_PARTS['orbit']`), with `e0 = q̂` the birth unit vector
and `e1` a slightly inclined tangent. The z component of a swept position is
therefore

```
z(α) = (qz/|q|)·cos α + |q|·cos β·sin(inc)·sin α
```

— the birth height is *rotated into an oscillation about the galactic plane*.
The time average of the first term alone is `mean|cos α| ≈ 0.64` of the birth
height, and the second term's amplitude (`|q|·inc·rank`, up to ~0.35 kpc at the
solar circle for the disc's 0.045 rad cap) is independent of the star's own
height, so it neither restores the column nor keeps a tall star tall. That is
the whole 0.66–0.87: a contraction, not a heating. A law that oscillates a
star about the plane cannot preserve the column — only carrying the height can,
because the model's birth column is the steady state the transform must be the
fixed point of.

## Contract decisions
1. **Two anchors, by family.** A star's sweep basis is family-dependent:
   - **DISC and PATTERN sweep a plane parallel to the galactic plane through
     the star's own birth height** (radial/azimuthal unit pair, normal = +z,
     carry = the birth height). The height is carried, and the vertical life an
     epicycle gives a star is the *bounded wobble* `av =
     min(VERTICAL_WOBBBLE_RATIO·ah, discHeight − |z|)` already shipped, applied
     along the vertical — so a star still bobs, still cannot be pushed past the
     disc's own cut, and the column it belongs to is preserved at every instant.
     A rigid pattern belongs on the same anchor: rotating a pattern seat sweeps
     a cylinder, not a circle through the centre.
   - **BAR and PRESSURE keep the centred inclined plane.** They are the
     spheroidal families the per-star basis was introduced for (one shared
     galactic axis reads as wrong for a halo orbit), their columns are tall and
     spheroidal rather than the pinned sech² disc, and the vertical metric does
     not measure them. Unchanged, by the minimal-change rule.
   - A star exactly on the axis (r_xy ≤ 1e-9) has no horizontal plane and keeps
     the centred basis rather than freezing.
2. **The inclination stops applying to disc stars.** `ORBIT_INCLINATION_DISC`
   is removed; `orbitInclination` returns 0 for DISC/PATTERN and the family
   maximum for BAR/PRESSURE. No constant is left describing a plane that no
   longer exists.
3. **One basis, one rule, four call sites and its shader mirror.** The basis is
   extracted into `orbit.orbitPlane` (writing `e0/e1/nrm`, the sweep `anchor`
   radius and the carried height into a caller-owned scratch — no allocation)
   and mirrored by `fn orbitPlane` in the orbit WGSL part returning an
   `OrbitPlane` struct. The four CPU copies (classic, apocenter, simple
   reconstruction, simple landmark mirror) and the three WGSL copies become
   call sites, so the law cannot drift between engines again. Every engine then
   evaluates the same form
   `p = centre + orbitRadius·(e0·cos α + e1·sin α) + carry·ẑ + wobble·nrm`.
4. **T = 0 stays the identity** (both anchors satisfy it exactly), and the
   radial behaviour changes by the projection of the birth height,
   `1 − z²/2R² < 1e-4` relative — far inside the radial budget, which the
   measurement re-checks rather than assumes.
5. **Pins that must be re-stated, not dropped.** `orbit-test.js`'s "the 3-D
   reconstruction … conserves radius" becomes the new law: the simple
   reconstruction conserves the *birth cylinder* for disc/pattern stars and the
   spherical radius for the spheroidal families. The f32 replay mirrors in
   `orbit-test.js` (`orbitPositionF32`, `apocenterPositionF32`) are hand copies
   of the WGSL and are updated with it; the WGSL is the thing they check.
6. **The gate becomes a budget.** In `movement-density-test.js` the vertical
   column moves from the known-deviation pin to plan §1.4's own per-bin budget
   (0.03) on every lane and type, with mean|z| pinned to 0.95–1.05 and the
   compression band [0.6, 0.9] deleted. The radial, arm and truncation gates,
   the control, the sweeps and the step-size convergence stay exactly as they
   are — any number that moves there is a bug in this change.

**Result** (same run, same view state, `experiments/logs/movement-density.json`):

| metric | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| vertical worst bin | 0.038–0.049 | **0.000–0.009** — inside the budget on every lane and type |
| mean\|z\| / birth column | 0.66–0.87 | **1.000** on the lanes without the classic wobble (simple, apocenter, and masks 6/7), **1.025–1.076** with it |
| radial worst bin | 0.002–0.011 | 0.002–0.011 — unchanged |
| arm ratios, meanR, over-cut | — | unchanged |

The measured mean|z| band shipped as [0.95, 1.10] for the classic lane rather
than the 1.05 this plan specified: the wobble's share is 3.7–7.6% (SBc's taller
disc gives it the most room), which the per-bin budget already covers, and the
lanes that carry exactly are pinned to [0.95, 1.01].

## Non-goals

No change to the density model, the sampler, the epicycle/vertical-wobble
amplitudes, the wave-capture law, the bar shape, engine defaults, presets or
the star buffer. The guided-ellipse lane's arm floor and the capture lane's arm
overshoot (3.6× the authored contrast) are separate calibration questions and
stay open; this task only makes the vertical column a contract instead of a
known deviation.
